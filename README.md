# Gradix

An academic companion for university students. Track your subjects, grades, attendance, and study tasks, with tools to calculate exactly where you stand and what you need next.

Built this because I needed a way to actually track where I stood in my own semesters at COMSATS, instead of guessing my GPA until the transcript showed up. It grew from a simple grade calculator into a full academic companion with attendance tracking, study task reminders, and home screen widgets, all running locally on the device with no accounts and no ads.

## Features

- Subjects built from Parts and Components, so your grade breakdown matches how your actual courses are structured
- Assessment dashboard with upcoming deadlines and quick add
- Progress view with visual breakdowns of subject and semester performance
- Study Tasks with reminders, so nothing slips through
- Home screen widgets for upcoming assessments and study tasks
- Notifications for upcoming deadlines and tasks
- Tools suite: Attendance Tracker, Subject GPA Calculator, Semester GPA Calculator, CGPA Calculator, and Improvement Effect Calculator
- Customizable grading scale and subject structure to match your university's system
- Light and dark theme with multiple accent colors
- Backup and restore your data anytime
- 100% local, no internet, no accounts, no ads
